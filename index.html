What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals use deceptive messages or websites to trick people into revealing passwords, OTPs or financial details.

Warning Signs

  • Unexpected urgent messages.
  • Suspicious links or misspelled domains.
  • Unexpected attachments.
  • Requests for passwords, PINs or OTPs.
  • Unrealistic prizes or offers.

Example: Suspicious Message

Your account will be blocked today! Click this link immediately to verify your details.

Do not trust a message just because it uses an official-looking logo. Verify it independently.

How to Stay Safe

  • Never share your OTP, PIN or password.
  • Check website domains carefully.
  • Use official apps and trusted bookmarks.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Update your browser and software.
  • Report suspicious messages.
